An interactive study of forecasting

We are terrible at predicting the future.
The pattern in how we're wrong is the interesting part.

For a century we've forecast flying cars, market plateaus, mass starvation and the end of history. Some calls were eerily right; most missed — but they missed in systematic, learnable ways. Scroll to explore how prediction has evolved, which futures are knowable, and where humanity keeps fooling itself.
